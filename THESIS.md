## Current view

1. **Spend is still accelerating.** Hyperscaler capex is growing faster than a
   year ago, and guidance points higher.
2. **It has outgrown internal cash.** Capex now absorbs most of the spenders'
   operating cash flow, so the marginal dollar comes from bonds, equity, leases
   and off-balance-sheet structures. This makes the build-out a credit story, not
   only an equity story.
3. **Suppliers are booking the money now.** Chips and memory revenue is
   surging. This is the most visible payoff so far.
4. **The macro payoff is in GDP, not yet productivity.** IT investment is a
   large share of recent GDP growth; productivity has not clearly stepped up.
5. **The market has split.** It pays suppliers and has stopped paying the
   spenders, which suggests equity investors doubt the return on the spend
   before credit investors do.
6. **The main risk channel is funding.** Higher long rates, heavy issuance and
   negative free cash flow at some spenders make funding cost and credit
   appetite the variables to watch.
